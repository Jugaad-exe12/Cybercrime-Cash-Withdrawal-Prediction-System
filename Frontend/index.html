About Us

Our system is a predictive analytics platform designed to support cybercrime investigation. It analyzes cybercrime complaint and transaction data to identify patterns and forecast likely cash withdrawal locations.

The main objective of this system is to help investigators identify potential high-risk locations in advance and support timely and proactive intervention using data analytics, machine learning and geographical visualization.
